# Iriza Ella Luxine ✦ Birthday Portal

A bespoke, luxury digital birthday sandbox space created specifically for **Iriza Ella Luxine**.

This platform is a highly polished, interactive birthday capsule designed for friends, family, and visitors to celebrate her golden milestone.

## Features
- **Capsule Dashboard:** Live celebration countdown, time-aware greetings, and custom vibe tracker.
- **Ella's Space Gallery:** Persisted curation of her photos and videos with interactive reactions, comments, and engagement tracking.
- **Memory Guestbook:** A high-contrast digital board where visitors can compose and pin heartfelt letters.
- **Chronicle & Comedy Board:** An interactive sandbox for inside jokes, sassy roasts, and floating visual emoji soundboard.
- **Checkpoints Timeline:** Activity tracker documenting milestone moments.
